# Design Principles and References

This document captures the visual psychology and design standards referenced throughout LayoutSense-X outputs.

## Reading Patterns
- **Z-pattern:** Guides attention across hero sections and towards CTA; suitable for balanced marketing pages.
- **F-pattern:** Matches scanning behavior on text-heavy or feed-like layouts.
- **Gutenberg Diagram:** Evenly distributes attention across a grid; useful for modular information blocks.

## Composition Rules
- **Rule of Thirds:** Place key subjects or CTAs at intersection points to increase focal strength.
- **Golden Ratio:** Use 1:1.618 ratios for harmonious sizing between major elements.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Tiered priority (1 = CTA/headline, 2 = hero imagery, 3 = supporting copy) to direct attention.

## Gestalt Principles
- **Proximity:** Group related elements to signal association.
- **Similarity:** Use consistent styling to tie related content.
- **Closure:** Allow whitespace to imply grouping without heavy borders.

## Color and Contrast
- Follow **WCAG AA** contrast guidance for text and CTA legibility.
- Apply **color psychology** tuned to audience (e.g., high-contrast blues for students, restrained palettes for trust-building).

## Marketing Alignment
- Map funnel stage to design emphasis: TOF (awareness, light CTA), MOF (education + CTA), BOF (conversion, strong CTA and social proof).
- Adjust density: high clarity for novices/students; stylistic latitude for designers.

## Reference Sources
- Nielsen Norman Group: Reading patterns and usability insights.
- AIGA: Professional layout and typographic standards.
- Google Material Design: Ratio and color contrast guidelines.
- Gestalt principle primers for grouping and hierarchy.
